Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for the apps
SOP 1: Every incident must be logged in ServiceNow with priority and category.
SOP 2: P1 incidents must be escalated to the on-call manager within 15 minutes.
SOP 3: Before restarting any production server, get change approval.
SOP 4: After the fix, update the ticket with the root cause and close it with user confirmation.
